Methodology & Sports Science

Rooted in Exercise Physiology & Workload Science

We replace guesswork with validated sports science frameworks, combining Foster’s session-RPE method with non-linear machine learning to detect acute overreaching before chronic fatigue sets in.

Foster Session-RPE Method

Calculates acute session volume by multiplying subjective exertion (Borg CR-10 scale) against total training duration, providing standardized internal load across varied workout modalities.

Acute-to-Chronic Baselines

Compares current microcycle workload against 7-session acute and 14-session chronic moving averages to identify sudden volume spikes—the leading predictor of soft tissue injury.

Autonomic & Lifestyle Telemetry

Integrates resting cardiovascular responses with sleep architecture, subjective recovery ratings, and psychological stress to differentiate adaptive strain from systemic exhaustion.

Explainable Class Attribution

Provides granular probability scores across Low, Moderate, and High fatigue states, pairing each prediction with key contributing factors so athletes know exactly what to modify.
